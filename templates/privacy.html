Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, along with the expense records, categories, and amounts you enter while using the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the expense tracking service — for example, to display your spending history, generate summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated session. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the application, and these providers only process data as needed to keep the service running.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
